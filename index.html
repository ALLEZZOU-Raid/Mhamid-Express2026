About the Rally

The M’Hamid Express is a legendary rally-raid event in the Draa-Tafilalet region. Known for its challenging desert tracks, breathtaking dunes, and vibrant community atmosphere, it brings together sports fans, car enthusiasts, friends, and families for an unforgettable adventure.

Event Program

  • Jan 25: Opening ceremony, scrutineering, prologue stage
  • Jan 26–29: Rally stages across dunes and desert tracks
  • Jan 30: Final stage, awards ceremony, closing party
